**About the role**::
We're looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join our Payments Platform team. You'll design and build backend services that process high-volume transactions for enterprise clients, working closely with product, infrastructure, and partner engineering teams. This is a hands-on role with significant ownership over service design and delivery.

**What you'll do**::
Design, build, and maintain scalable backend services and APIs
Partner with product and platform teams to translate requirements into technical solutions
Participate in code review, architecture discussions, and on-call rotation
Improve observability, reliability, and performance of production systems
Mentor mid-level and junior engineers

**Required qualifications**::
* 5+ years of professional software engineering experience
* Strong proficiency in Java, Python, or a comparable backend language
* Experience designing and consuming RESTful APIs
* Experience with relational databases and SQL
* Hands-on experience with a major cloud provider (AWS, Azure, or GCP)
* Experience with CI/CD pipelines and automated testing
* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field, or equivalent practical experience

**Preferred qualifications**::
* Experience with event-driven architecture and message brokers (Kafka, SQS, or similar)
* Experience with containerization and orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes)
* Infrastructure-as-code experience (Terraform, CloudFormation)
* Background in financial services, payments, or another regulated industry
* Experience with observability tooling (Datadog, Grafana, Prometheus)
* Prior experience mentoring engineers or leading small technical initiatives

**What we offer**::
Competitive compensation, comprehensive health coverage, 401(k) match, and a hybrid work model.
